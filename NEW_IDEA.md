## Idea: Automated Strategy Marketplace

A new feature for Polyblocks: a marketplace where users can publish, discover, and copy trading strategies. Users can rate, review, and subscribe to strategies, and creators can optionally monetize their work. This will require:
- User profiles and strategy publishing
- Strategy discovery/search
- Copy trading integration
- Ratings, reviews, and analytics
- Optional monetization (subscriptions, revenue share)
